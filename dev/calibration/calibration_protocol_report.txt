  * The SELECTED line is the model this protocol chooses. It is not necessarily

  * The gate reads interval LOWER bounds, so a pass here is harder than the
    point estimates the earlier rounds reported. A point estimate above 90% with
    a lower bound below it means the corpus is too small to prove the claim yet,
    not that the model got worse.
