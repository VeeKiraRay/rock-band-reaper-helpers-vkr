Reading this report:
  * The SELECTED line is the model this protocol chooses. It is not necessarily
    the highest mean - a bigger model has to beat a simpler one across repeats,
    not just on average once.
  * The usable and miss gates read interval bounds at the pessimistic end, so a
    pass on them is harder than the point estimates the earlier rounds reported.
    A point estimate above 90% with a lower bound below it means the corpus is too
    small to prove the claim yet, not that the model got worse.
  * RHO IS GATED ON ITS PACK-BOOTSTRAP LOWER BOUND as of 2026-08-22, so every
    figure the gate reads is now the pessimistic end. It was gated on the MEAN
    until then, because ten correlated reruns over the same songs have no honest
    interval. The split range printed beside it is still repeat-to-repeat spread
    and still is NOT a confidence interval - read the bound, not the range.
  * These are DEVELOPMENT-SET figures and the Wilson bounds are not confirmatory
    intervals. The same repeated-CV results both choose the candidate and report
    its quality, so the bound on the selected model is optimistic by an unmeasured
    amount. No reserved test partition has been drawn, and none can be drawn from
    these rows - see PackIsReserved in protocol.lua.
  * MAE is deliberately absent: it is not comparable between the rank and
    log(rank) scales, and comparing it across them would pick the wrong model.
