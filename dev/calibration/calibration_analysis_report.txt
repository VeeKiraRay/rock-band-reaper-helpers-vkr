Pass condition: usable (perfect+good) >= 90%, misses <= 5%, and rho >= 0.70
The rho condition exists only to rule out hitting the usable% by predicting near
the middle of the range. Exact-tier agreement is NOT a pass condition: Harmonix
set these ranks with playtesters, and two charts of identical density can differ
in how hard they feel, so exact agreement was never the realistic target.
